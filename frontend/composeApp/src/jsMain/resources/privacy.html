Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 8, 2026

This policy describes how BudgeYet ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information for users of the BudgeYet mobile and web applications.

Information We Collect

BudgeYet is a collaborative household budgeting app. To provide its core features, we collect:

  • Account information: your email address and a 6-digit PIN that you choose at signup. Your PIN is never stored in plain text — it is hashed before being saved.
  • Profile information: your display name and/or nickname.
  • Household data: household membership, roles, invites, and currency settings for households you create or join.
  • Budget and transaction data: budgets, category limits, and transactions you or other household members enter, including amounts, merchants, dates, and payment mode.

We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or any other tracking technology in the app.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account and authenticate you.
  • To provide the household budgeting features you use: shared budgets, category limits, transaction tracking, and household member management.
  • To send you account-related emails: PIN recovery codes and household invites.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-Party Services

We use Resend to deliver transactional emails (forgot-PIN codes and household invites) on our behalf. Resend processes the recipient email address and message content solely to deliver these emails.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your account and household data are retained for as long as your account remains active. If you would like your account and associated data deleted, contact us at hello@imhx.top and we will process your request.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including hashing PINs and restricting access to household data to members of that household.

Children's Privacy

BudgeYet is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at hello@imhx.top.
